=== map / filter / reduce ===
map(x*2): [2, 4, 6, 8, 10]
filter(偶数): [2, 4]
推导式更 Pythonic: [2, 4, 6, 8, 10], [2, 4]
reduce(+): 15

times5(4) = 20
times3 的类型: function

  3 - 4 = -1
  3 * 4 = 12
  3 / 4 = 0.75
  3 ^ 4 = 81
